This brief covers AI news from 2026-10-06 UTC.

Money moved fast today: DeepSeek blew past its raise target, Reflection shipped a frontier open model, and China's top court set the rules for AI disputes.

## DeepSeek nears $15B raise ahead of 2027 IPO

**What happened:** DeepSeek is close to securing at least 80 billion yuan (about $15 billion) in a Tencent-backed round, past its roughly 50 billion yuan target, with CATL and Tencent committing among the largest amounts. Term sheets could push the total near 100 billion yuan ahead of a planned early 2027 IPO.

**Why it matters:** A funded DeepSeek with a 160, 000-chip Huawei data center planned in Inner Mongolia means cheaper open-weight competition keeps coming for anyone pricing inference.

[Source: The Straits Times](<https://www.straitstimes.com/business/deepseek-set-to-raise-at-least-15-billion-in-tencent-backed-funding>)

## Reflection debuts Beam, a 501B open-weight model

**What happened:** Reflection AI unveiled Beam, a text-only mixture-of-experts model with 501 billion total and 23 billion active parameters, a 1 million token context window, and 23.8 trillion training tokens. Reflection claims it matches Z.ai's GLM-5.2 on advanced reasoning benchmarks using 3-4x less inference compute, though the claims are not independently verified.

**Why it matters:** A Western open-weight model claiming frontier reasoning at lower token cost gives teams another self-hostable option for coding and agent workloads.

[Source: TechCrunch](<https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/reflection-debuts-beam-a-open-weight-ai-model-to-rival-chinese-models-at-lower-compute-cost/>)

## Cohere ships North 2 with new agent harness

**What happened:** Cohere released North 2, a redesigned orchestration system for multi-step enterprise agents with memory, libraries, skills, artifact and app creation. It is model agnostic, supports cloud, on-premises and air-gapped deployments, and adds admin cost controls, rate limits, user quotas and org-wide caps.

**Why it matters:** Granular cost caps and air-gapped deployment options matter for teams that need agents running inside regulated or private infrastructure.

[Source: Cohere](<https://cohere.com/blog/introducing-north-2>)

## GLM 5.3 lands on Amazon Bedrock for enterprises

**What happened:** Z.ai's GLM 5.3, a 753B-parameter mixture-of-experts model optimized for coding and long-horizon agentic tasks, is now on Amazon Bedrock through managed APIs with cross-Region inference, prompt caching and service tiers. Access is limited to eligible enterprise customers.

**Why it matters:** Managed access to a 753B open-weight coding model removes the provisioning work for teams that want agentic capability without running their own inference stack.

[Source: Amazon Web Services](<https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/introducing-glm-5-3-on-amazon-bedrock/>)

## China's Supreme Court sets AI dispute framework

**What happened:** The Supreme People's Court published 24 articles on trying AI-related disputes, covering tort liability, IP disputes, procedural and evidentiary rules and criminal liability. Fault-based liability is the default, and copyright infringement liability is shared among developers, providers and users based on fault.

**Why it matters:** Anyone shipping AI into China now needs governance, contractual safeguards and evidence preservation across the model lifecycle to manage fault-based exposure.

[Source: Reed Smith](<https://www.reedsmith.com/articles/china-s-supreme-court-issues-landmark-opinions-on-ai/>)

## OpenAI opens opt-in text watermarking for API users

**What happened:** OpenAI set out its text watermarking approach under the EU AI Act, opening opt-in watermarking to API customers worldwide from October 5, with the setting off by default. Invisible watermarks will reach eligible ChatGPT and Codex text output in the EU in coming weeks, and applications opened for a limited detector.

**Why it matters:** API watermarking is opt-in and off by default, so builders shipping into the EU should decide now whether to enable it for their own transparency obligations.

[Source: Unite.AI](<https://www.unite.ai/openai-begins-phased-text-watermarking-under-eu-ai-act-rules/>)

## XPeng raises over $900M for robotics and Physical AI

**What happened:** XPeng's robotics business signed share purchase agreements raising over $900 million at a post-money valuation above $6.3 billion, led by IDG Capital with Gaorong Ventures and strategic support from Tencent and Alibaba. The funds go to R&amp;D, Physical AI model training and production facilities.

**Why it matters:** Capital at this scale for humanoid robotics signals growing demand for the on-device inference and training tooling that Physical AI stacks depend on.

[Source: Evertiq](<https://evertiq.com/news/2026-10-06-xpeng-raises-over-900m-to-accelerate-physical-ai-deployment>)
