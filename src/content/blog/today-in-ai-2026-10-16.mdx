This brief covers AI news from 2026-10-16 UTC.

Meta's new open-weight coding model lands on Blackwell Ultra, Cohere signs to buy a security startup, and Arm shows off agentic rack hardware.

## Meta opens 816B Muse Spark for agentic coding

**What happened:** Meta released Muse Spark Open, an 816B mixture-of-experts coding model with 42B active parameters and a 1M token context window, trained on NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 Blackwell Ultra. NVFP4 checkpoints from Meta shrink the memory footprint of all 816B parameters with native FP4 Tensor Core acceleration.

**Why it matters:** A 1M token window holds an entire repo, so agentic coding tools can plan multifile refactors and recover from failing tests without chunking the codebase.

[Source: NVIDIA](<https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/scale-agentic-coding-workflows-with-metas-muse-spark-open-on-nvidia-gb300-nvl72/>)

## Cohere signs letter of intent to buy Aisle

**What happened:** Cohere said it signed a letter of intent to acquire Aisle, a San Francisco cybersecurity startup that uses AI to find software vulnerabilities. Terms were not disclosed. Aisle founder Ondrej Vlcek will lead Cohere's new cybersecurity unit, and Aisle's 50 employees will join.

**Why it matters:** Aisle says its AI found more than 500 bugs in widely used software in its first year, including 12 in OpenSSL, so Cohere is buying vulnerability detection for its enterprise stack.

[Source: Reuters](<https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/cohere-moves-buy-ai-cybersecurity-startup-aisle>)

## Arm puts its AGI CPU into production racks

**What happened:** At the OCP Global Summit 2026 in San Jose, Arm showed production systems, customer traction and an open agentic rack architecture built around Arm AGI CPU, its first production silicon, introduced in March at Arm Everywhere. Arm also published a new methodology for measuring agentic workloads plus independent benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** Rack-level designs and published benchmarks give infrastructure teams a way to compare agentic serving hardware before committing to a buildout.

[Source: Arm](<https://newsroom.arm.com/blog/key-takeaways-from-ocp-global-summit-2026>)

## IFM and Inception42 launch Jais 3 for Arabic

**What happened:** The Institute of Foundation Models, part of Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence, and Inception42 introduced Jais 3, six open language models from 900 million to 375 billion parameters plus Jais Speech for Arabic and its dialects. All are built on K2 Horizon, IFM's open-source foundation model fleet launched last month.

**Why it matters:** Open weights across a wide size range let teams in the Middle East and North Africa run Arabic speech and agent workflows on their own hardware.

[Source: Institute of Foundation Models](<https://ifm.ai/jais3/press-release/>)

## Scale Labs benchmarks agents that optimize agents

**What happened:** Scale Labs published VeRO, an evaluation harness that benchmarks coding agents on agent optimization by treating the target agent program as the search space. Across 105 optimization runs spanning five benchmarks, it found tool-use tasks admit meaningful optimization, while current models show limited exploration diversity and a bias toward prompt edits over architectural changes.

**Why it matters:** If your agent harness is hand-tuned, VeRO gives a way to measure whether a coding agent can actually improve it, and where that loop breaks down.

[Source: Scale Labs](<https://labs.scale.com/blog/vero>)

## Modal raises $750M Series D at $15.75B

**What happened:** Modal raised a $750 million Series D at a $15.75 billion post-money valuation, co-founder Erik Bernhardsson wrote in a company blog post. Accel led the round, with Kleiner Perkins and existing investors Menlo Ventures, General Catalyst and Lux Capital participating. Modal recently crossed $1 billion in annualized run rate.

**Why it matters:** More capital for serverless GPU infrastructure means another scaling option for teams that do not want to run their own inference clusters.

[Source: AI Early Edition](<https://aiearlyedition.com/brief/2026-10-15/>)
