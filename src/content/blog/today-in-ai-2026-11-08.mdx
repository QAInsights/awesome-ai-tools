This brief covers AI news from 2026-11-08 UTC.

Money moved at the top of the stack today, while a security shop found credentials sitting in public repos after a Claude Code permission prompt.

## Fireworks hires Meta veteran Dave Wehner as CFO

**What happened:** Dave Wehner, who spent eight years as Meta's finance chief, is leaving the company to become CFO of Fireworks. Fireworks was valued at $17.5 billion in July and is in early talks about another funding round.

**Why it matters:** A CFO hire and fresh round talk at an inference provider usually precede pricing and capacity changes for teams serving open models through it.

[Source: AI Affairs](<https://www.aiaffairs.com/>)

## Fal raises $1.3B at a $16B valuation led by Bessemer

**What happened:** Fal, a San Francisco startup running video, image and audio models, raised $1.3 billion at a $16 billion valuation. Bessemer Venture Partners led, with Nvidia, Sound Ventures and T. Rowe Price joining.

**Why it matters:** More capital for a media-model inference vendor means builders get another well-funded endpoint to route image and video generation through.

[Source: Axios](<https://www.axios.com/pro/enterprise-software-deals/2026/11/07/enterprise-software-deals-fal>)

## Ant Group ships Ling-3.2-flash with a 1M-token window

**What happened:** Ant Group released Ling-3.2-flash, a native multimodal MoE with 560B total and 25B active parameters, a 1M-token context and text, image and video input. Chat and OpenAI/Anthropic-compatible APIs are live and free for two weeks; weights stay closed.

**Why it matters:** Compatible endpoints plus a two-week free window make it cheap to test long-context multimodal calls without rewriting client code.

[Source: Neural Daily](<https://www.neuraldaily.news/en/models>)

## Apiiro: Claude Code prompt left passwords in 1, 122 public files

**What happened:** Application-security firm Apiiro says in a blog post that Claude Code's "Don't Ask Again" permission choice exposed passwords in 1, 122 public GitHub files. Anthropic says the file is meant to stay local.

**Why it matters:** If your agents can write outside the repo, audit which permission prompts your team has silenced before the next push.

[Source: AI Tracker](<https://ai-tracker.rootstudio.io/news>)

## OpenCode Browse lets an agent drive your real browser

**What happened:** OpenCode said on November 6 that OpenCode Browse is available. It lets an agent control the user's own browser, with their open tabs, logins and extensions, working through MCP and a Chrome extension.

**Why it matters:** Agents inheriting live sessions and logins remove the auth scaffolding from browser automation, and remove the sandbox with it.

[Source: AI Tracker](<https://ai-tracker.rootstudio.io/news>)

## NVIDIA posts four open Cosmos 3 Nano world models

**What happened:** NVIDIA released four 16B-parameter Cosmos 3 Nano open-weight checkpoints for interactive world simulation, covering multi-sensor driving data, streaming observations, simulator-to-RGB transfer and bimanual robot action-to-video.

**Why it matters:** Open weights at 16B let robotics and simulation teams fine-tune a world model locally instead of licensing one.

[Source: Neural Daily](<https://www.neuraldaily.news/en/models>)

## Scale Labs tests coding agents on drug discovery tasks

**What happened:** Scale Labs ran Claude Code (Opus 4.7), Codex (GPT-5.5) and Gemini CLI (Gemini 3.1 Pro) on 66 expert-curated biomedical tasks in a shared tool environment. GPT-5.5 led structural reasoning, Opus 4.7 led patent mining, and the longest pipelines defeated all three.

**Why it matters:** Long-horizon tool chains, not single answers, are where coding agents still break, which is where your orchestration and retries matter most.

[Source: Scale Labs](<https://labs.scale.com/blog/coding-agents-drug-discovery>)
